# Developer Play Voxlet 

The play voxlet is intended for development prototyping, bug hunting and so on.

## Why do you need a Play Voxlet?
   We use it internally to test new things, and reproduce bugs.
   
## Why is it called Play?
   The Play Voxlet embodies work-in-progress. And **play** is preferable to **work**.
   
## Can I try the play Voxlet?
   The Play Voxlet exists just for testing. Usually it's hidden from Users.
